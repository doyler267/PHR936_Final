How the replacement calculation works

Your three original unit-exam scores and both parts of the final are counted first. If—and only if—the cumulative final score is higher than your lowest unit-exam score, the lowest score is then replaced. In symbols: Exam 1 + Exam 2 + Exam 3 + cumulative + non-cumulative + replacement gain, where the replacement gain is the amount by which the cumulative score exceeds the lowest unit exam. The maximum is 200 points.

Before the cumulative score passes your lowest unit-exam score, a cumulative point and a non-cumulative point have the same effect. Above that mark, each additional cumulative point adds two course points—one on the final and one through replacement. Use the sliders to compare any combination you want. Your instructor’s official gradebook and syllabus remain the final authority.

This planning tool estimates exam points only. Confirm course policies and rounding rules with your instructor.
